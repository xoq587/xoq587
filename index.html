Help center

How do I contact XOQ?

Email hello.xoq@outlook.com. Please do not send passwords, payment details or private conversation screenshots.

Is XOQ available yet?

XOQ is in release preparation. Availability will be announced once the app is ready.

What if an AI reply does not feel right?

AI suggestions can misunderstand context. Review every reply before using it, and never feel obliged to send a suggestion.

What if a screenshot will not upload?

Check photo access in your device settings and your internet connection. If the issue persists, contact support with your device model and app version, without attaching private conversations.

How do I manage an Apple subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Select XOQ if listed. For purchase questions, contact support without sharing payment credentials.

Can I request a refund?

For Apple-billed purchases, visit Apple’s refund request page. Apple determines eligibility.

Safety center

XOQ targets users aged 13 and older, subject to applicable local age requirements. Age-appropriate safeguards are planned and must be tested before access is opened to minors. Do not use XOQ to harass, exploit, manipulate or pressure others. Never share intimate images of minors. If someone pressures or threatens you, seek help from a trusted adult or appropriate local service. XOQ is not an emergency service.
